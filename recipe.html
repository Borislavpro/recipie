Loaded BBQ Chicken
& Potato Bowl

A comforting blend of crispy golden potatoes and succulent BBQ chicken, topped with a zing of sour pickles.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Boneless, skinless chicken breast, cubed
  • 2-3 medium Potatoes (Russet or Yukon Gold), cubed
  • 1 large Onion, sliced thinly
  • Barbecue sauce (to preference)
  • Sour pickles (optional, for garnish)
  • Kosher salt, black pepper, and garlic powder

Cooking Method

01

Preparation

Preheat your oven specifically to 400°F (200°C). Ensure a baking sheet is ready for the potatoes.

02

Roast Potatoes

Combine cubed potatoes with olive oil, salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Roast for 25-30 minutes until crispy and golden brown.

03

Cook Chicken & Onion

Sauté cubed chicken and sliced onion over medium-high heat until chicken is cooked through and onions are caramelized.

04

Combine & Simmer

Reduce heat to medium-low. Pour in BBQ sauce, stir to coat, and simmer for 3-5 minutes to meld the flavors.

05

Assemble & Serve

Create a base of crispy potatoes. Spoon the saucy BBQ chicken over them. Top with sour pickles for a sharp contrast.

Enjoy your meal!
